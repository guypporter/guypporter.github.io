Guy Porter

Guy has a background in investigations and data analysis for campaign organisations Global Witness, Avaaz and Purpose, and training and development for the Centre for Investigative Journalism (CIJ), Public Media Alliance and Tactical Tech.

Working across projects focused on climate, technology and human rights, he uses open source data and tools to support advocacy campaigns and investigations that focus on people, power and the planet.

His work has been published in the UK and internationally by The Guardian, The Times, Coda Story, Crikey, RTE and Deutsche Welle.

Open Climate Reporting Iniative (OCRI)

Organisation: Centre for Investigative Journalism (CIJ)

The Open Climate Reporting Initiative (OCRI) is an initiative of the CIJ. It focuses on raising the standard of environmental investigations to enable thoroughly researched public interest reporting and evidence-based advocacy through training and skill-sharing with target audiences across six different regions. I led on the development of the curriculum for using Open Source Intelligence (OSINT) for environmental and climate reporting and delivered a number of workshops as part of the international programme.

Digital Investigative Toolkit

Organisation: Public Media Alliance

The goal of this Public Media Alliance project was to ensure that broadcast journalists in Sub-Saharan Africa feel empowered by new technology and use it to continue to deliver powerful reporting that drives change. Working in collaboration with local journalists, I designed a digital investigations toolkit to complement the wider programme of events.
